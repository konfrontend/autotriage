# Letter template: audit

Assessment is the deliverable. The Letter sells a bounded, paid review and names what the client holds at the end of it. The build that follows is one sentence, never priced.

Slot notes for this Family:

- `deliverables`: a list only for three or more distinct artifacts; fewer, and the block is prose that names them in its first sentence and runs straight into `follow_on`. When the Job lists what the review should cover, answer that list in our own words, one artifact per line, never an echo of the client's items. When the Job has no list, four lines: findings ranked by severity, the map of what to keep, fix, or rebuild, effort and order for the next stage, assumptions and deferred items. `follow_on` is the closing paragraph of this block.
- `relevant_work`: two Past Jobs by default. Prefer ones where an existing system was read and judged.
- `follow_on`: one to three sentences. What the review does not touch, why the deliverable that carries a Mitigation gets its own hours, and how the next stage continues from the list; no second engagement shape.
- `billing`: capped hours for the assessment, billed on actual, unused hours not billed. No rate unless the Job states neither budget nor rate.
- `stack`: only when the Job asks for a proposed stack.
