The Letter is filled, not written: the Family template orders the Slots, the Slot catalogue says what each holds and how big it may be, the voice rules say how it sounds, and every fact comes from the Profile. This State maps the Job's demands to Slots, fills the Slots in template order, writes `letter.md`, and shows the Letter. `verify` reviews it afterwards; this State applies the rules while filling so `verify` has little to fix. Everything printed here besides the Letter (demand map, fact table) is session output and is thrown away.

Inputs, read all before step 1: `data/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/job.md` (posting verbatim in the body; `verdict.family` and `verdict.flags` in the frontmatter), the Profile (`data/profile/profile.md` for `identity`, `terms`, `word_cap`; `data/profile/skills.md` for the Skill rows with `level` and `evidence`; `data/profile/jobs/*.md` bodies as the only source of facts about the freelancer; `data/profile/voice.md` for `greeting`, `sign_off`, `banned_extra`, `allowed_extra`, `cite_role_period`), the Family template `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/catalogue/templates/letter-<family>.md`, the Slot catalogue `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/catalogue/templates/letter-slots.md`, the voice rules `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/catalogue/voice.md`, and the Flag catalogue `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/catalogue/flags.md` for the `mitigation` and `slot` of each triggered Flag. Output: `letter.md` next to `job.md`; contract in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/catalogue/candidate-artifacts.md`, schema in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/schemas/letter.schema.json`.

## Steps

1. **Demand map.** Walk the whole Job body and list every explicit request the client makes of the proposal (examples, a stack, an approach, availability, subcontracting, a specific question; never a keyword demand, which the Letter ignores). Assign each to the Slot that answers it per the Slot catalogue; what no Slot answers goes to `answers`, in the Job's order, with a header worded from the demand. When the Job lists questions or asks (screening questions, `please include ...`), the Letter is structured by them: hold each as a header, a real question verbatim with numbering stripped, a request fragment reworded in first person from the freelancer's side (never `you`/`your` in a colon header), and note which Slot renders under it (Slot catalogue, `Ask headers`); the question asking for availability is the last block before `closing` and carries `terms`, plus the rate and `billing` when it asks for those. Screening questions (a block introduced as questions to answer when submitting) are demands: map each one. Decide each `when: job asks` Slot of the template from the map: active only with a demand pointing at it. Then add the Mitigations: for every id in `verdict.flags`, look up the Flag; a risk Flag with `slot` other than `none` puts its `mitigation` on the map against that Slot (`plan` is the Family's `plan_slot`). Done when every explicit request and every Mitigation has a Slot, and every `when: job asks` Slot has a decision.

2. **Facts.** For every active Slot that cites the freelancer (`relevant_work`, `gaps`, `stack`, `answers`, `terms`, `billing`), pick the facts from `jobs/<slug>.md` bullets and `profile.md` fields. Choose Past Jobs by match to the Job's requirements, the number the Slot catalogue and template set. Gaps come from the map: a required item of the Job (a must, a required skill, the codebase) with no `shipped` row in `skills.md` is a gap, and the nearest Past Job bullet is what is named against it. A preferred or nice-to-have item is never a gap; with no required gap, `gaps` renders nothing. Hours, prices, totals, and timezone overlap are derived from `terms` and `identity` and marked `derived`; `derived` covers arithmetic on Profile numbers only, never a claim about the freelancer (availability, hours reachable, willingness to show code) that no Profile field states. Hold a fact table: fact, Slot, source (`jobs/<slug>.md` bullet, `profile.md` field, `job.md` for client facts, `derived`). A demand or Slot that needs a fact the Profile lacks (a concrete thing done, a role, a product URL): ask the human for every such fact in one message, naming the Past Job it would belong to, and wait. A confirmed fact is appended as one bullet at the end of the matching `jobs/<slug>.md` body with the Edit tool before filling continues; a fact the human denies or does not know is dropped and the Slot is filled without it. Done when every row of the fact table has a source and no question is open.

3. **Fill.** Fill the active Slots in template order, each by its Slot catalogue entry, the template's Slot notes for this Family, and the voice rules. The Hook restates the Job's goal and constraint within the `hook` Slot `cap.chars` and opens with a phrase of understanding; with Flag `mandatory-keyword` triggered, the keyword appears nowhere in the Letter. The plan Slot carries the Mitigations mapped to it, in its own words; `billing` and `answers` carry theirs. When the plan costs more than the posted budget, `billing` states both figures and what the posted budget buys. Done when every active Slot has content or an explicit reason to render nothing, and every mapped demand and Mitigation is stated in its Slot.

4. **Render.** Assemble the plain text: the Profile `greeting` on line 1, a blank line, then the Slots in template order, one block each, one blank line between blocks. Headers from the Slot catalogue `headers` on their own line with no blank line before the content, replaced by the ask headers from step 1 for the Slots an ask points at, ask blocks in the Job's order after the plan block and the availability ask last, right before `closing`; `follow_on` as the last paragraph of the plan block; `sign_off` alone on the last line. Then read the text against the voice rules once as a whole: prose words under Profile `word_cap` with header, list, and `answers` lines excluded, total under the voice catalogue `total_word_ceiling`, every Slot within its Slot catalogue `cap`, one list at most, only `https://` URLs, no banned phrase or pattern, no Markdown. Trim the longest prose Slot first. Done when the text passes that reading.

5. **Write `letter.md`** with the Write tool at `data/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/letter.md`: frontmatter `slug` (from `job.md`), `family` (from `verdict.family`), `date` (from `job.md`), `outcome: null`; then the Letter text. The write hook validates the frontmatter against the schema and blocks with the error list on mismatch: fix and write again. Done when the write went through.

6. **Question blocks.** Every question from step 1 is in `letter.md` under its header from step 1; nothing is answered outside `letter.md`. Done when each question has its answer in the file.

7. **Show.** Print, in this order: the demand map, one line per demand as `demand -> Slot`, with the `when: job asks` decisions and the Mitigations placed; the Past Jobs chosen and why, one line each; the fact table. No Letter here: the human sees it only after `verify` has passed it. Done when the three are on screen.

8. **Hand off.** Invoke `autotriage:verify $ARGUMENTS` with the Skill tool, no question in between. Its report ends with the verified Letter. Done when `verify` has run.
